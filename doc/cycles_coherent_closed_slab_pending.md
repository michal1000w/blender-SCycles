# Closed Glass slab: independent pending acceptance

Actual editable scenes and numerical references are in `tests/output/diffraction/coherent_closed_slab_pending_v45_exact`. One closed finite n=1.5 Glass object separates an exterior point source from an exterior unit Lambertian detector. Two coincident 1 mW sources provide phase0/π/distinct-group factors4/approximately0/2. The saved float phase and layer thicknesses are used. This is **pending production support**, not a rendered refraction acceptance result; v45 streamed mirror mode rejects declared Glass.

For parallel layers, Snell's law conserves p=n sinθ. The independently derived radial mapping is r(p)=Σdᵢp/√(nᵢ²−p²), with derivative Σdᵢnᵢ²/(nᵢ²−p²)³ᐟ². The launch-solid-angle to detector-area Jacobian is p/[√(1−p²) r dr/dp], obtained from dΩ=2πp dp/√(1−p²) and dA=2πr dr. Its normal limit is1/(Σdᵢ/nᵢ)². Exterior air on both ends means no additional net eta² factor is applied on top of this angular mapping. Fresnel power transmittances multiply separately for s and p; unpolarized intensity averages the two. OPL isΣnᵢdᵢ/cosθᵢ.

The test-only oracle checks the Jacobian against finite annulus solid-angle/area ratios, the homogeneous n=1 limit cosθ/R², the central limit, and finite-difference Fermat derivative dOPL/dr=p. At r=.01m and nominal .01m layers, p=.354037648564, OPL=.036821204295m and geometric Jacobian1193.62282839m⁻². Independent single-source radiance is.02784806350 before source interference. Only primary TT is referenced under the declared two-interface budget; higher internal-reflection histories are excluded. Pixel-center references are staged; actual renderer gates/quadrature acceptance remain unregistered until production supports this route.

[PBRT's Snell/Fresnel treatment](https://www.pbr-book.org/4ed/Reflection_Models/Specular_Reflection_and_Transmission) and [dielectric solid-angle/radiance discussion](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Reflection_Models/Dielectric_BSDF) support the underlying interface laws. The multilayer radial Jacobian above is an independent derivation, not a quoted PBRT formula. No production runtime changes were made and the broader arbitrary-refraction goal remains incomplete.
