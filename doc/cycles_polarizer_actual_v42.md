The [actual native Metal report](../build/tests/python/native_polarizer_render_v42/report.json) passes all 39 checks across PT, BDPT and PT guiding. The [actual PT and BDPT photographic triplets](../tests/output/diffraction/polarizer_actual_v42/actual_polarizer_contact.png) are complete and visually inspected. Both modes show the white rectangular reflection retained by the glare-pass orientation and strongly reduced by the crossed glare-cut orientation, with the checker floor visible underneath. They use the same finite area emitter, Glass slab, colored checker floor and physical camera analyzer, at fixed AgX EV0 and 256 samples. All six raw EXRs are finite and undenoised; [gallery provenance and editable scene links](../tests/output/diffraction/polarizer_actual_v42/index.html) preserve actual raw hashes.

The fixed top-origin glare ROI x270:370, y190:290 has descriptive raw linear-luminance ON/OFF ratios 0.921710 (pass) and 0.014865 (cut) in PT, versus 0.921785 and 0.014793 in BDPT. Adjacent checker ratios are approximately 0.606 and 0.375 in both modes. These are descriptive measurements, not new acceptance gates. The finite source spans a range of incidence angles, so the photograph is not equated with a central-ray Brewster formula or used to fit an exposure. OFF uses legacy scalar dielectric transport; ON enables polarization throughout the scene. Background changes across multiple Glass bounces cannot be interpreted as isolated analyzer transmission, and no energy or PT/BDPT brightness identity gate is imposed. No suspicious mode-specific visual difference was observed at this resolution; Monte Carlo noise and finite-area residual reflection remain visible.

All [48 coherent Metal cases pass their prospective physical gates](../build/tests/performance/coherent_polarizer_render_v42/prospective_gates.json): seven source-side Malus variants, five Brewster-reflection variants, and four camera-side detector/filter variants across PT, BDPT and guiding. The maximum absolute mean error is 1.028e-8; maximum RMSE is 2.338e-8. Camera-side single and parallel films reproduce one half of Lambertian detector radiance; crossed films have a numerical residual mean approximately −2.56e-10, below the predeclared 1e-8 leakage gate. Every report retains raw EXR, worker, scene and independent-reference hashes. Earlier partial reports remain archived.
