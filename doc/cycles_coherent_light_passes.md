# Signed coherent surface light passes

Coherent interference does not uniquely belong to one classical path. The supported decomposition uses an explicit accounting convention: each diagonal belongs to its route, and each signed cross pair contributes one half to each of its two routes. At the first camera-visible marked Lambertian detector, a source route without an optical interface is Diffuse Direct; a route with one or more reflected/refracted interfaces is Diffuse Indirect. Either pass can be negative under destructive interference. Their sum remains the original completed-path intensity, without independently clamping or training guiding with the allocated terms.

If the camera path already scattered before reaching the detector, all new radiance is indirect. The native first-camera-scatter diffuse/glossy/transmission throughput fractions choose the corresponding Indirect passes. This accounts for an unmarked camera glass or reflective suffix without classifying the incoming illumination as a camera-visible glossy lobe. Volume suffixes and shadow catchers remain unsupported.

The evaluator's Combined accumulation order is preserved. Pass computation is requested only when light passes are enabled, and film writes signed radiance without using the ordinary individually clamped direct-light writer. Native pass extraction divides the stored lighting radiance by the corresponding Color pass. The independent compositing identity is therefore:

`Combined RGB = Diffuse Color*(Diffuse Direct+Diffuse Indirect) + Glossy Color*(Glossy Direct+Glossy Indirect) + Transmission Color*(Transmission Direct+Transmission Indirect) + Emission + Environment`.

Supported output additions are the diffuse/glossy/transmission aggregate, direct and indirect passes plus Emission and Environment. Existing surface-data passes remain supported. Lightgroups, volume-light decomposition, AO and shadow-catcher decomposition are still rejected. This does not remove the model's geometry, material, radiometry or native-control limits.

`tests/performance/cycles_coherent_passes_test.cpp` checks the convention against independent complex fields, including negative Direct with positive total intensity, all first-camera-lobe choices and RGB Color recomposition (154 checks). `tests/python/cycles_coherent_light_passes.py` authors primary and secondary editable fixtures without rendering. The primary has colored Lambertian response and both direct and planar-reflected source routes. The secondary adds an unmarked ideal glass window in the narrow camera aperture; source illumination misses the finite window. Its renderer gates require unchanged same-seed Combined, finite full passes, the compositing identity, both primary diffuse classes, and secondary transmission-indirect attribution with zero Direct. Actual renderer acceptance remains pending until its saved report passes; CPU algebra alone is not pipeline validation.
