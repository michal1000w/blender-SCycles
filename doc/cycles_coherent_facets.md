# Streamed mirror facets

The opt-in **Streamed Mirror Facets** transport model replaces direct illumination and up to two ideal reflections from participating sources at marked Lambertian detectors. It streams every triangle of every declared Mirror mesh, including separate objects and mesh instances. There is no 64-facet or 64-route limit. Select **Max Interface Events = 1** for direct and one reflection, or **2** to include every ordered two-reflection triangle pair. The existing **Bounded Interfaces** model remains the default.

Surfaces are exact geometric triangle facets, with flat shading and no custom normals. This is geometrical optics on a faceted surface, not a smooth curved reflector or unrestricted wave solution. Material and detector restrictions of coherent transport remain: ideal white mirrors and passive Lambertian receivers. Refraction, rough scattering and chains longer than two reflections are outside this mode; their existing native incoherent estimators remain active. Marked Glass and sphere interfaces are rejected in this mode.

Each triangle uses an exact planar image-source reflection and physical, compensated segment optical length. Triangle membership bounds the stationary point. Native robust offsets affect visibility rays only; any interior BVH intersection is a blocker. No optical length is computed from offset rays. Exact shared edges have one directed-edge owner. The point-source model, scalar mirror option and three independent world-axis dipole Jones model are those of bounded coherent transport. Color remains an RGB radiometric envelope at the declared phase carrier, not monochromatic spectral color reconstruction.

For each coherence group and camera sample, one shared standard Gaussian variable Z modulates every path by exp(i Z (L-Lanchor)/Lc). Its expected path-pair factor is exp(-(La-Lb)^2/(2 Lc^2)), exactly the existing Gaussian coherence contract. Thus the field squared is an unbiased stochastic estimator of that model; finite coherence adds Monte Carlo variance. Source-group validation requires identical carrier wavelength and coherence length. Groups are summed independently. Compensated complex sums preserve cancellation; compensated modular phase retains sub-cycle phase for large optical differences and tiny coherence lengths. Subnormal coherence lengths below 32 FLT_MIN in scene units are explicitly unsupported.

Direct/indirect passes use the existing signed half-cross attribution. Per sample, direct = Re(D conj(D+R)); indirect = Combined-direct. Signed pass values are intentional and are not clamped. Combined is a nonnegative field norm per group. Source identity and the actual typed participating mirror hit establish ownership; a reflection beyond the selected one/two-event limit or another scatter invalidates that prefix. BDPT terminates owned light prefixes at their first marked receiver. Unmarked and longer histories retain native transport.

One reflection costs O(triangles × sources); two reflections cost O(triangles² × sources), with constant field storage (864 bytes for the three-mode RGB sums). This does not promise high throughput for huge meshes. Facet edges, grazing boundaries and caustic/edge diffraction require different wave models.

Focused numerical evidence is `build/tests/performance/coherent_facet_stream_v44/results.json`: strict/fast CPU geometry, 83-route finite-Gaussian expectation against independent double pair sums, infinite-coherence complex sums, signed pass reconstruction, cancellation, tiny-Lc phase and typed ownership over 80 instances. The v44 single-reflection package passed 27 actual Metal cases against independent 80-facet scene references. The two-reflection increment has separate focused evidence in `build/tests/performance/coherent_facet_stream_v45/results.json` and passed nine corrected scene cases plus three default-specialization checks in v45. See [the versioned delivery report](cycles_diffraction_delivery_v45.md) for unchanged gates, the preserved fixture-budget failure, exact backend settings and actual images.

Two-reflection paths use exact ordered image-source unfolding, barycentric membership at both physical hits, and three BVH-tested segments. Distinct triangles on the same object are permitted, and linked instances remain object-qualified. A repeated identical triangle is excluded. Each physically valid route contributes to the same shared Gaussian field sum. Camera, glossy, source and BDPT depth budgets are checked at depth two before summation; native prefix ownership uses that same selected depth. Near a corner where reflection order switches, compensated physical segment lengths retain phase without iterative solver dropout.
